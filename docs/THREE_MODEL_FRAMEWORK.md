# The Four-Tier Framework — capacity, conversion, pressure, scarring

**The Modernization program now has four complementary instruments**, each measuring one
face of the founding equation (**rupture = interior complexity > interface capacity**), and
each — proven empirically — owning a *different* class of outcome at a *different* timescale.
Together they span the space: what a polity *is*, how well it *delivers*, how much *pressure*
it is containing, and what damage the *ground* already carries.

| | **V1 — Capacity** | **V2 — Conversion** | **V3 — Pressure** | **Tier 4 — Scarring** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Asks** | what is its structure? | does it deliver, and for whom? | is pressure building? | what happened on this ground before? |
| **Measures** | 5 pillars, durability gap | 6 conversion pillars (Level + Equity) | grievance + mobilization + opportunity | conflict history (decayed) + trust + displacement + state-perpetration |
| **Timescale** | generational | experiential | political-cycle | **civilizational (carries history forward with decay)** |
| **Predicts (validated)** | the **ancient rupture** (armed conflict ~0.72); structural trajectory across 5,000y | **wellbeing** (life-sat via balance, t=−3.06) | the **modern rupture** — backsliding (0.54→0.61), repression (0.64→0.71) | **conflict recurrence** (+0.092 AUC over V1+V3 → **0.88**) |
| **Does NOT predict** | modern backsliding (≈chance) | any political outcome | the trigger | which scarred states heal (decay rate is measured; the trajectory is contingent) |
| **Status** | frozen, validated (109 cases, 15 findings) | construct-valid; equity null; wellbeing signal | gate-passed; deep-validated 1816–1996 | all hypotheses passed; **operationalizes the conflict trap** (mediates the raw binary) |

**The one law measured on both sides, across four timescales.** V1/V2 are the **capacity
denominator**; V3 is the **live complexity numerator**; **Tier 4 is the *accumulated* numerator
— the pressure history leaves in the ground.** Each owns a distinct rupture: V1 the ancient
(violence from weakness), V3 the modern (backsliding from contestation), Tier 4 the recurrent
(violence from scarring). Tier 4's headline result is that it turned the program's most stable,
least-explained relationship — the conflict trap (ρ +0.21..+0.67 across two centuries) — into a
measurable, decomposable, **25-year-half-life** construct that *fully absorbs* the raw
prior-conflict binary. Societies heal from violence on a generational, not civilizational,
timescale — one of the framework's open questions, now answered quantitatively.

**The equation, fully instantiated.** V1 and V2 are the **capacity denominator** (structure
and its delivery); V3 is the **complexity numerator** (organized pressure). The whole
program's physics — rupture is the interior outrunning the interface — is now measured on
*both* sides for the first time. And the striking empirical result is that **capacity
predicts the ancient failure mode (violence) timelessly, while the complexity tier is
required for the modern one (backsliding), which only emerged as an anocracy-and-civil-
society configuration in the last third of the 20th century.** The instruments were never
wrong; each reads the rupture of its era.

## Complete results ledger (2026-07 session)

### V1 robustness — the convergence arc
- **Finding 15 — wealth catching up, CONFIRMED (complementary facet).** The 150-year
  "institutional erosion" is GDP *gaining* crisis-predictive power, not institutions losing
  it — confirmed via frozen pre-registration + adversarial audit. Coupling rises on
  like-for-like samples; sector composition shifted (agriculture 24%→10%); GDP predicts
  crises worse where farming dominates. Mechanism is *complementary facet*, not pure proxy
  (both P1 and GDP stay significant jointly). Audit downgraded the crisp "institutions flat"
  claim to "robust direction, fragile p-values" and "only resource decouples" to "not
  significant." `docs/CONVERGENCE_RESULTS.md`, ROBUSTNESS_RESULTS Finding 15.
- **Finding 16 — WGI ceiling-bias, systematic NULL but heterogeneous.** WGI does not
  uniformly overstate decline at the top; but **Netherlands/UK declines are largely
  perceptual** (WGI slipped while tax revenue + education spending rose), while **US/Germany
  decline on both perception and revealed function** — real. 21/35 WGI decliners flip to
  stable on revealed tax. `docs/` Finding 16.
- **Capital-suppression thesis — TESTED and RETIRED (reversal).** The claim that the modern
  financial system is an engineered suppression accumulating a proportional release **failed
  its first pre-registered test and reversed**: longer-held wealth-above-institutions gaps
  have *smaller* subsequent collapses (ρ=−0.22, negative across all 18 cells). Retired to
  historical context across the atlas + grand synthesis. `CAPITAL_SUPPRESSION_*`.
- **Divergence scan.** 112 of ~198 economies show wealth rising while institutions
  stagnate/decline; 91 in active decline (US #34, −7.3). The EU-27 as one body diverges when
  population/GDP-weighted (ΔP1 −1.1/−2.4) but not one-country-one-vote (+1.7) — enlargement
  averaged Western erosion against Eastern accession gains. Finding 16 caveats it (NL
  perceptual, US real). `divergence_scan_full.json`, `eu_body_divergence.json`.

## What the three capture together — and what they can't

- **V1** reads *structural capacity* → the ancient rupture (violence) + the slow structural
  trajectory. Timeless, deep (5,000y), the validated core.
- **V2** reads *delivery* → wellbeing (via balance). A valid construct whose political
  ambition failed but whose wellbeing signal is real.
- **V3** reads *pressure* → the modern rupture (backsliding, repression). Calibrated risk.

Together they instrument both sides of the founding equation across capacity, conversion, and
complexity — the fullest measurement of a polity's structural condition the program can make.
**What none of them capture — and cannot — is the trigger.** The spark that converts pressure
into rupture (a self-immolation, a stolen election) is in no dataset before it happens. That
is the **Mule**, and it is the physics of the ceiling: three models raise prediction from
chance to calibrated risk over bands, and then stop. Honestly stated, that boundary is what
makes the rest credible — this is structural seismology, not a Seldon Plan, and it now has
three seismographs where it had one.
