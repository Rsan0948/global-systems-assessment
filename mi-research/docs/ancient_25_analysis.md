Hindsight is unavoidable (outcomes known) → every test is a *post-hoc consistency check*, not a forecast.

## Where I DISAGREE with the source doc
The doc's headline is "P1-ordinality is the most vulnerable finding — high-P1 polities collapsed."
**Run honestly, that dissolves into two errors:**
1. **Timepoint error.** The framework predicts from the **pre-stress** state, not the peak. Sasanian
   Persia: peak P1=5 but **pre-stress P1=3, P5=1** — already collapsing (succession chaos, plague,
   war-exhaustion) *before* the Arab conquest. So it **confirms container-erosion**, it does not refute
   ordinality. Measured at the right timepoint, the "high-P1 collapse" vanishes.
2. **Known scope, not a new refutation.** The remaining "challenges" — Achaemenid (Alexander), Song
   (Mongols) — are **exogenous conquests**, where P1 stops discriminating (everyone ruptures, 2/2 high
   and 4/4 low). That is the *same* exogenous/idiosyncratic-shock scope limit moderns already carry
   (Mod8; the Chile/South Korea misses). It is not pre-modern-specific and not a refutation.

**Net: P1-ordinality travels to antiquity exactly as far as it travels in modernity — strong for
endogenous stress, defeated by exogenous conquest/plague. The doc over-claimed its failure.**

## Where the doc is RIGHT — genuine challenges & extensions
- **Venice (convergence-closure):** institutions can be simultaneously *durable* (P1 stability high,
  unchanged) and *extractive/ossifying* (P4 dynamism falls 5→3 post-Serrata). The convergence qualifier
  conflates "stability-preserving closure" with "growth-preserving openness" — a real refinement.
- **Wari (centralization-as-trigger):** P1 *rising* (2→3) preceded decline — inverts "institutions
  rising = developmental." Small/contested, but a genuine edge case.
- **Migrate/transform as a THIRD outcome:** Angkor and Aksum *relocated* their economic core rather than
  rupturing or absorbing — the binary absorb/rupture container model needs a third state.
- **Ruler-dependence ("bus-factor"):** Maurya (post-Ashoka), Mali (post-Suleyman), Majapahit (post-Gajah
  Mada) — performance lodged in individuals, not institutions. This is the **ancient durability concept**
  (granted-by-a-person vs earned-by-institutions), and it maps onto the P4−P1 gap (high output, thin
  institutions).
- **Confederacy fragility:** Sasanian (Parthian houses) and Aztec (tributaries) dissolved with startling
  speed once coercion lapsed — = the suppression typology where the "container" *is* coercion.

## Where I DISAGREE that the extension is universal
- **The environmental moderator is real but NOT universal.** Climate/hydraulic/trade shocks drove
  outcomes in Khmer, Classic Maya, Old Kingdom, Aksum — but **Tiwanaku's Bayesian redating (Marsh 2023)
  puts the collapse BEFORE the drought** — a social/ritual cascade, not climate. So the doc's "add an
  environmental moderator" is right as a *conditioner*, wrong as a universal driver; ritual/ideological
  legitimacy failure (Tiwanaku, Maya divine kingship) is the deeper load-bearer in several "climate" cases.

## The actual prize: cross-temporal corroboration
The two findings that hold from **2686 BCE to 2024** — the **durability gap (6/6)** and **container
erosion (10/10)** — are the strongest possible evidence that they are *deep structural regularities*,
not modern-data artifacts. A pattern that survives from Old Kingdom Egypt to the eurozone is the closest
this project gets to a law. (Caveat below — these are my ordinal reads with hindsight; the consistency is
real but the test is not blind.)

## Honest limits
- **Scores are mine and interpreter-dependent** — the central weakness ("strong heuristic, weak oracle").
  A different scorer could shift borderline cases; the *direction* of the strong findings is robust to
  reasonable re-scoring, the exact counts are not.
- **Hindsight contamination** — outcomes were known when scoring; treat as consistency-check, not forecast.
- **Literacy bias** — textual empires (China, Persia, Rome) score more confidently than archaeology-only
  cases (Tiwanaku, Wari, Great Zimbabwe), which risks the framework looking universal because well-
  documented states resemble each other. The 7 MODERATE + archaeology-dependent cases are directional only.
- **Firewall absolute:** ancient tier is never merged into the validated quantitative corpus; it informs
  hypotheses (esp. the environmental conditioner, migrate/transform outcome, ruler-dependence), nothing more.
