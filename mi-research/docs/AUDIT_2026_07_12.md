# Adversarial Audit + Hardening — 2026-07-12

Two independent adversarial audits over the multi-tier political-signal / CSD code, plus a
hardening pass. The founding rule of this program is that **the graveyard is the map**: findings
that don't survive scrutiny are reported as prominently as those that do. Several headline
numbers from the tier-5/6/deep-panel work were **statistically compromised** (in-sample AUC,
cross-country confounding, pseudoreplication, non-reproducible CV) and have been corrected here.
Regression suite: `tests/test_tiers.py` (now encodes the corrected numbers). All corrected
scripts are deterministic run-to-run.

## CRITICAL — fixed

- **C1a — `deep_political.py` reported IN-SAMPLE AUC as "out-of-sample".** `logit_auc` fit and
  scored on the same rows (resubstitution → AUC→1.0 at small n_pos). The "numerator beats
  structure across two centuries, **+0.131**" was an overfitting artifact. **Fix:** stratified
  K-fold OOF (`cv_logit_auc`), in-sample kept only as a labeled diagnostic. **Honest result:
  backsliding OOF +0.071 (positive 6/11 epochs); conflict OOF +0.033.** Direction survives,
  magnitude ~2× inflated.
- **C1b — `tier6_spark.py` reported IN-SAMPLE AUC for every headline.** Full six-tier "AUC
  **0.712**" and all increments were resubstitution, mechanically rising with predictor count on
  n≈114. **Fix:** OOF `cv_auc`, both reported (`tier6_auc.json`). **Honest result: six-tier
  OOF 0.63; T6 adds +0.15 over T5 OOF (real); T5 alone near-chance OOF (0.48); T5×T6 interaction
  n.s. (z=−1.07).**
- **C2 — `tier6_spark.py` non-reproducible / crash-on-fetch-fail.** Border adjacency fetched live;
  a failed fetch crashed (`csv.DictReader(StringIO(None))`) and an upstream change would silently
  reshape every T6. **Fix:** committed, version-pinned `_borders_cache.json`; graceful offline
  fallback; snapshot only overwritten when inputs are valid.

## HIGH — fixed / corrected

- **H1 — pseudoreplication** in `csd_deep`/`trigger_hunt`: overlapping windows counted as
  independent → p-values wildly anti-conservative. **Fix:** `csd_corrected.py` uses **one
  observation per backsliding episode** and a **country-clustered bootstrap**.
- **H2 — `trigger_hunt` "variance countdown" (AUC 0.689) is a confound.** Cross-country (volatile
  poor states rupture more) + mechanical coupling (variance of libdem elevated before a defined
  libdem move); the theory-canonical AR1 indicator is null/negative. **Corrected:** within-country
  variance elevation is **real (81% of 240 episodes, country-clustered CI excludes 0)** but is
  **not a monotonic countdown**; **AR1 is NULL (AUC 0.51, p=0.33)**.
- **H4 — `tier4` S2 (distrust) not percentile-normalized** while S1/S3/S4 were → scale mismatch
  inflating scarring for WVS-surveyed (stabler) states. **Fix:** 5/95 normalize S2. Snapshots
  reconciled (mean 1.9-pt T4 shift, e.g. USA 56→50).
- **H5 — `tier4` gate imputed missing V3 covariates as raw 0** (= the *minimum* for exclusion),
  biasing the baseline and inflating the increment. **Fix:** mean-imputation. **Net result: the
  Tier-4 increment rose to +0.112, CI[0.070,0.153], 100% seeds positive — still PASS.**
- **REPRO — set-iteration order was hash-randomized** in `political_test`, `tier4`, `deep_political`
  (`set(A)|set(B)`), so single-seed CV fold splits — and the published gate AUCs — changed every
  run. **Fix:** sorted iteration everywhere + 50-seed repeated CV with CI (M5).

## MEDIUM — fixed / noted

- **M4 — `political_test` T1 "partial-out" was DEAD CODE** (`rz`/`rx` computed, discarded), so
  survivors were uncontrolled marginals. **Fix:** real partial-Spearman net of P1+logGDP + a
  second BH-FDR pass. **Honest survivors: anocracy+cso→backslide, pts/prior-conflict/youth→
  conflict; internet/ethnic-exclusion/food-import were confounded and drop out.**
- **M5 — single-seed CV, no CI** → fixed by 50-seed repeated CV (above).
- **M6/M7/M8/M9 (noted, not yet fixed):** v2 equity triple-counts income-Gini (3/6 pillars);
  tier4 T4-6 "mediation" is partly collinearity (raw_prior ≈ S1 by construction — the direction
  is right but "mediation" overstates it); tier6 S1 has spatial-autocorrelation leakage into its
  univariate AUC; tier6 `haveborder` unused so ISO-mapping gaps masquerade as island insulation.
  Tracked for a follow-up pass; none change a headline conclusion.

## LOW — noted

- **H3** — hand-coded countries missing from the snapshot (AZE/LBY/SOM/SYR/VEN) silently dropped,
  gutting tier7 archetype **A6 to n=1** (IRQ only); tier7 was already the program's weakest/most-
  null tier (M1 lift +0.01) and is reported as such. L10 (tier7 LOO standardization leak), L11
  (tier6 `nf` precedence latent crash), L12–L15 (labeling/normalization edge cases) tracked.

## Net effect on the program's claims

The **capacity/pressure/scarring** core is intact and, after honest re-estimation, **stronger and
now reproducible**: V3 backsliding gate +0.093 CI[0.033,0.156]; Tier-4 conflict-recurrence +0.112
CI[0.070,0.153]. The **timing tiers were over-sold**: Tier 5 is a real *within-country turbulence*
signal, not an accelerating countdown, and near-chance as a cross-country predictor; Tier 6 adds
out-of-fold but the six-tier 0.712 was in-sample (true OOF 0.63). The deep-panel "+0.131 across two
centuries" is honestly +0.071 OOF. Every affected results doc carries a correction banner.
