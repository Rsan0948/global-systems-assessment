# Exploratory: the MI-raw vs modifier-adjusted DELTA — is there signal?

**Date:** 2026-06-28. Kicking a tire: does the gap between a country's naive MI (equal-weighted pillar
mean) and its modifier-adjusted MI (after Safeguard E rentier discount + Safeguard J durability cap)
carry signal? EXPLORATORY; no engine change; baseline untouched.

## Result: real signal, but ~90% it's the durability gap we already have
- delta separates crisis vs no-crisis at **Cohen d=+2.27** (crisis countries get ~5× the discount) —
  BUT **corr(delta, P4−P1 durability gap) = +0.90**, so the delta is mostly **Safeguard J re-expressed**,
  and the d=2.27 is largely J re-confirmed on overlapping cases, not independent new signal.
- The modifier-**adjusted** MI barely out-separates raw MI (d −2.69 vs −2.60) → as a *score correction*
  the modifiers add almost nothing; their value is the **flag**, not a better number.

## The one genuinely useful artifact: headline-MI × delta (2×2)
delta is only partly correlated with the level (corr with MI_raw = −0.39), so it carves distinct cells:
- **Hidden-fragile** — high headline MI (≥0.60) + big modifier discount (≥0.04): **Cuba, Dominican
  Republic, Mexico, Belarus, Turkey.** Where the naive index *most overstates* durability (mid-tier
  scores propped up by income beyond what institutions sustain). Face-valid (known backsliders/strained).
- **Honestly poor** — low MI + low delta: Yemen, Afghanistan, Mali, Niger, Burkina Faso, Ethiopia,
  Uganda. Low but *institutionally consistent* — not propped up, arguably more predictable than a
  high-delta peer.
- (high MI + low delta = genuinely durable; low MI + high delta = visibly fragile.)

## Verdict
The tire is mostly sound but doesn't hide a new gauge: the modifier delta ≈ the durability gap, so it
adds no fresh predictive variable. The keeper is the **interpretive lens** — "where does the headline
MI most mislead?" → the hidden-fragile cell — which is cheap (headline × delta, both already computed)
and could be surfaced as a one-line diagnostic if wanted. Honest limit: the predictive d is largely
Safeguard J re-confirmed on overlapping cases, not independent validation; rents add only marginally
beyond the gap.
