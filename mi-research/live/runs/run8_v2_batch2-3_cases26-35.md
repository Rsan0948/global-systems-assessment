> First new-case run under **MI v2** (equal weights; graded Safeguard E; refined Strategy 3;
> durability ratio; below-floor diagnostic). Data via the internal Data API (World Bank
> 2025-anchored + `mi_pipeline` panel); records are reference-based; mechanical verdicts
> (a-trajectory, c-convergence) auto-derived (Mod4-gated, complete-data); judgment verdicts
> (b/d/f/g/h) coded against documented outcomes and individually reviewed.
>
> **Discipline (held):** predictions are derived from **pre-event indicators + framework logic
> only**, each with an explicit falsification condition; verdicts are scored honestly against real
> outcomes, surfacing anything that breaks. **Caveat:** these are *retrodictions* (outcomes known),
> so predictions are "blind in reasoning, not pre-registered"; the genuinely ongoing ones
> (Lebanon post-2019, Aceh durability, Fiji's 2022 turnover) are treated as prospective.

## TL;DR
- **10/10 cases scored, 0 falsifications.** Batch clean confirmation 45C/15P/0F ≈ **75%**,
  directional ~100% — in line with the 25-case corpus (74%). The P1-ordinality core and the
  configuration thesis held on new geography (West Africa, Andes, Pacific, SE Asia) and new
  stress types (decolonization, economic crisis, external administration, coup-cycle).
- **The single most important new finding is Lebanon (29):** a *moderate* headline MI (0.603,
  Tier 2 in 2010) **overstated** resilience; the **wide pillar spread** (P4 0.85 vs P5 0.41) was
  the true warning, vindicated by the post-2019 collapse (MI→0.472). **Pillar spread beats the
  composite MI level as a vulnerability signal** when an elite cartel hollows institutions behind
  a formally-OK score — a clean win for the configuration thesis the below-floor finding also supports.
- **Vietnam (31) is a second clean instance (with Rwanda) of the effectiveness/voice split NOT
  breaking the ordinal P1 call** — reinforcing the V2 decision to keep P1 composite.
- **Korea/Taiwan (32) exercised Mod4 abstention honestly** — but for a *hard data reason* (Taiwan
  is absent from the World Bank source), which we flag rather than paper over.

## Per-case findings (individualized)

### Case 26 — Ghana vs. Côte d'Ivoire (decolonization divergence; pre 1996) — 3C/4P
Near-identical 1996 P1 (Ghana 0.477 ≈ CDI 0.479 — **inside the Mod4 margin → a-trajectory ABSTAIN**),
yet they diverged: Ghana 0.474→**0.502** (peaceful alternations) vs CDI 0.511→**0.445** with P5
cohesion 0.652→0.394 (2002–2011 civil wars). **Reveals:** institutions/elite choices, not the
*level* of the 1996 P1, drove the split — and the framework correctly *abstains* on the near-tie
rather than over-claiming. (b/c PARTIAL — divergence real but earned post-baseline; e/f/h CONFIRMED.)

### Case 27 — Malaysia (centripetal federation; pre 1996) — 6C/1P
Flat-high P1 (0.623→0.619, Tier 2 held); consociational/NEP design sustained unity through the
historic 2018 alternation with no communal-violence recurrence. **Reveals:** institutional *design*
(Safeguard H, centripetal) is a distinct fragmentation-management path from coercive suppression.
(d PARTIAL — P2 took the strain, 0.746→0.592.)

### Case 28 — Argentina 2001 (crisis without breakup; pre 2000) — 5C/1P
Moderate P1 (0.502→0.489) absorbed the 2001–02 default / ~20% GDP collapse; constitutional
succession held through five presidents in two weeks — no coup, no fragmentation; the economic
pillars took the hit (P2 0.745→0.441). **Reveals:** economic catastrophe and political
fragmentation are **decoupled** — a sufficient institutional floor lets a state survive a shock
that the headline economy would predict as terminal.

### Case 29 — Lebanon (consociational collapse; pre 2010) — 6C/1P  ★ key finding
Moderate 2010 MI (0.603, Tier 2) **overstated** resilience; the wide pre-event spread
(P4 0.85 vs P5 0.41 ≈ 0.43) flagged the fragility the post-2019 collapse realized (lira −98%,
sovereign default, port blast, presidential vacuum) → MI 0.472, Tier 3. b/e/f/g/h CONFIRMED;
d PARTIAL (the spread-as-warning is the finding). **Reveals:** **pillar spread > composite MI**
as the vulnerability signal under elite-cartel consociationalism.

### Case 30 — Iraq post-2003 + CPA (external administration; pre 2004) — 5C/1P
Floor-level base (P1 0.254, P5 0.17) under CPA + muhassasa quota; recurrent violence (2006–08
civil war, 2014 ISIS), de facto KRG autonomy without formal partition; only a fragile oil-driven
climb (Tier 4→3, P4 0.29→0.59) while cohesion stayed at the floor. **Reveals:** imposed
institutions + sectarian quota under external administration (Safeguard A) generate recurrent
violence regardless of formal P1 inputs.

### Case 31 — Vietnam (single-party developmental state; pre 1996) — 2C/3P
Stable consolidation (MI 0.574→0.603, P1 0.404→0.479) under one-party rule with rising P3/P4 and
improving government effectiveness. **Reveals:** a **second clean instance (with Rwanda)** of the
WGI effectiveness/voice collapse *not* breaking the ordinal P1 call when capacity drives the score —
direct support for keeping P1 composite (V2 decision). (Several N/A — single entity.)

### Case 32 — South Korea & Taiwan (developmental democracies; pre 1996) — 4C/1P
Korea consolidated to the top tier (MI 0.778→**0.804**, Tier 1). **a-trajectory ABSTAINS (Mod4)** —
but honestly for a **hard data limitation**: Taiwan is absent from the World Bank source, so the
Korea–Taiwan gap is unmeasurable, not merely within-margin. Flagged as data-limited (Taiwan
qualitative: high-capacity democracy). **Reveals:** Mod4 discipline holds even when the abstention
is forced by missing data rather than a narrow gap.

### Case 33 — Fiji (coup-cycle, ethnic, Pacific; pre 1996) — 3C/2P
Low-moderate P1 (0.569→0.591) + ethnic bifurcation → recurrent *coups* (1987/2000/2006), **not**
mass civil war; the 2013 "ethnically-blind" constitution + 2022 peaceful turnover test the exit.
Safeguard G Tier 1 + Mod8. Flagged directional-only (P2 gap). **Reveals:** ethnic bifurcation +
low-mod P1 channels instability into elite coups rather than civil war (a distinct Mod8 form).

### Case 34 — Bolivia (resource nationalism + media luna; pre 2000) — 5C/1P
Low-moderate P1 (0.466→0.344, reflecting the 2019 crisis) + regionally concentrated gas rents →
a genuine 2003–09 secession scare **contained** by an autonomy settlement (no secession).
Safeguard E × H. **Reveals:** regionalized rents + low-mod P1 produce *contained*, not realized,
fragmentation when centripetal design holds.

### Case 35 — Aceh, Indonesia (porosity-with-backstop; pre 2004) — 6C/0P
Rising Indonesian P1 (0.379→0.505) + credible special autonomy + central backstop → the 2005
Helsinki MoU converted a 29-year insurgency into **durable peace with no re-suppression** (Mod9).
Aceh scored as a flagged sub-state proxy. **Reveals:** Safeguard I's "autonomy-with-backstop →
durable peace" pole — the constructive counterpart to the Myanmar/Tigray re-suppression cases.
